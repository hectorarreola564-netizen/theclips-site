Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This page explains how "The clips" (the YouTube channel @Theclips-p4u) and its companion application use data. This is a single-person, non-commercial automation project operated by its creator to publish the channel's own content.

1. This website

This site is static and informational only. It does not use cookies, does not run analytics or tracking scripts, and does not collect any personal information from visitors.

2. The upload application

A private application, authorized only by the channel owner, uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to automate publishing on this one channel. It requests the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • youtube.upload — to upload videos produced for this channel.
  • youtube.readonly — to read this channel's own video and channel metadata.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — to read this channel's own performance analytics (views, retention, subscriber growth) for internal reporting.

These scopes are used exclusively to manage the owner's own YouTube channel. The application is not a multi-user service: no other person authorizes it, and it does not act on any account other than the channel owner's.

3. Data we do not collect

We do not collect, store, or share viewer personal data, payment information, or any data belonging to people other than the channel owner. No data obtained through the Google APIs is sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and none is used for advertising.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

This application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Content and sourcing

Every video published on this channel discloses that its narration is a synthetic (AI) voice, and cites a verifiable public source for its central fact in the video description.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the project evolves. Material changes will update the "Last updated" date above.
