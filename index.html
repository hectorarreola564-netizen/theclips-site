YouTube Shorts

The clips

@Theclips-p4u — one country fact, every day.

"The clips" is a daily YouTube Shorts channel about countries and geography. Each 20–45 second video covers a single, fact-checked fact about a country, with a cited source linked in every video description.

Watch on YouTube

How it's made

Videos are produced with an automated pipeline: a synthetic narration voice (clearly disclosed in every description), original code-generated motion graphics and maps, and royalty-free or public-domain photos with credit given in the description. No stock footage with restrictive licenses, no copyrighted music.

Sources

Every fact cites a verifiable public source (government statistics, encyclopedias, or recognized data organizations), linked directly in the video description.
